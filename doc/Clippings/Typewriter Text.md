### The Quick Method (Using Standard Fusion Titles)

1. Go to the **Effects Library** on the Edit page, look under **Toolbox > Titles**, and drag a standard **Text+** title onto your timeline.
2. Select the clip, click on the **Fusion** page tab at the bottom of DaVinci Resolve.
3. In the Inspector on the top right, look at the text box where you type your words.
4. Right-click *inside* the text entry field (or just below it) and look for the option **Animatable Text** or add a **Modifier**.
	- *Alternatively:* In the Inspector, right-click the **Styled Text** field name itself and select **Follower** (this opens up advanced text animation modifiers).
5. The easiest path for a typewriter effect is using the **Typewriter** preset if available, or using a **Reveal** modifier:
	- Select your Text+ node, press `Ctrl + Spacebar` (or `Cmd + Spacebar` on Mac) to open the Select Tool window, type **Typewriter**, and hit Enter.
		- This instantly applies a fully procedural typewriter modifier to your text.

### Adjusting the Effect

Once the Typewriter node or modifier is active:

- **Control Speed:** You can adjust the keyframes in the Fusion timeline or use the Inspector controls to dictate how fast or slow the letters reveal themselves.
- **Cursor:** You can often toggle a custom blinking cursor character (like `_` or `|`) at the end of the typing sequence for that authentic terminal look.

It saves you a trip into Blender if you just need a crisp, clean 2D text overlay for your video introduction or code walkthrough notes!
